# Google Forward Deployed Engineering Interview Prep
# Google Forward Deployed Engineering 面试准备

This repository documents my preparation for the **Google Forward Deployed Engineering (FDE)** interview process. It serves as a structured collection of notes, timelines, strategies I employed,  practice problems, and reflections across all major interview components.
本仓库记录了我准备 **Google Forward Deployed Engineering (FDE)** 面试过程的详细信息。它汇集了我在各个核心面试环节中的笔记、时间线、所用策略、练习题以及反思总结。

## 📌 Overview
## 📌 概览

The Forward Deployed Engineering role blends software engineering, problem-solving, and customer-facing skills. This repo is organised to reflect the multi-dimensional nature of the interview process:
Forward Deployed Engineering (前瞻部署工程) 角色融合了软件工程、解决问题以及面向客户的能力。本仓库的组织结构反映了该面试流程的多维特性：

* Timeline & Recruiter Screening (时间线与招聘人员初步筛选)
* Data Structures & Algorithms (DSA) (数据结构与算法)
* Vibe Coding (practical, collaborative coding) (氛围编程 - 考察协作与实际编码)
* Googleyness (behavioral & culture fit) (行为准则与文化契合度)
* Agentic & Machine Learning System Design (智能体与机器学习系统设计)

---

## ⏳ Timeline & Recruiter Screening
## ⏳ 时间线与招聘人员初步筛选

Focus: Mentioning of past experiences, RRK and Why Factor?
重点：提及过往经历、相关角色认知 (RRK) 以及“为什么选择这里”的因素。

**Contents (内容包含):**

* What interests you in this role? (你对这个角色有什么兴趣？)
* Tell me about your past experiences? (Prepare 1 Agentic Model & 1 Classical ML Example) (讲讲你过去的经历？准备 1 个智能体模型例子 & 1 个传统机器学习例子)
* 30 - 45 mins where you should ask questions more around the Team and the recruiter too (30 - 45 分钟的交流时间，你应该多问一些关于团队和招聘人员的问题)
* What are you pursuing outside of work? (工作之余你在追求什么？)
* End-To-End Interview Process takes 6-8 Weeks including self-preparation (包含自我准备在内，端到端的整个面试流程大约需要 6-8 周)

## 🧠 Data Structures & Algorithms (DSA)
## 🧠 数据结构与算法 (DSA)

Focus: Problem-solving under constraints, code efficiency, and clarity.
重点：在约束条件下解决问题、代码效率以及清晰度。

**Contents (内容包含):**

* Core topics (arrays, trees, graphs, DP, etc.) (核心主题：数组、树、图、动态规划等)
* Practice problems with solutions (附带解答的练习题)
* Patterns and heuristics (模式与启发式方法)
* Time/space complexity breakdowns (时间/空间复杂度分析)
* Mock interview notes (模拟面试笔记)

**Goal:** Build strong intuition and the ability to communicate solutions clearly. 
**目标：**建立强大的直觉，并具备清晰沟通解决方案的能力。

## 💻 Vibe Coding
## 💻 氛围编程 (Vibe Coding)

Focus: Real-world coding in a collaborative, fast-paced environment.
重点：在协作、快节奏的环境中进行真实的编码工作。

**Contents (内容包含):**

* End-to-end coding exercises (端到端编码练习)
* Code readability and structure (代码可读性与结构)
* Debugging strategies (调试策略)
* Working with ambiguous requirements (处理模糊需求)
* Communication during live coding (实时编码中的沟通)

**Goal:** Demonstrate practical engineering skills and adaptability.
**目标：**展示实际工程技能和适应能力。

## 🤝 Googleyness Interview
## 🤝 Googleyness 面试

Focus: Behavioural traits aligned with Google's values.
重点：符合 Google 价值观的行为特质。

**Contents (内容包含):**

* STAR method responses (STAR 原则回答技巧)
* Past experience breakdowns (过往经历深度拆解)
* Leadership, ownership, and teamwork examples (领导力、主人翁精神以及团队合作的例子)
* Handling ambiguity and failure (应对模糊性和失败)
* Reflection logs (反思日志)

**Goal:** Show thoughtful decision-making, humility, and impact.
**目标：**展现深思熟虑的决策能力、谦逊和影响力。

## 🤖 智能体与机器学习设计

Focus: Designing intelligent, scalable systems with ML components.
重点：设计包含机器学习组件的、智能且可扩展的系统。

**Contents (内容包含):**

* System design fundamentals (系统设计基础)
* ML system architecture (机器学习系统架构)
